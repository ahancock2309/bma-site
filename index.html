The journey

Most operators are one step from owning their business

A job is a business with one client: your boss. Going self-employed gives you more clients. Hiring a team makes you an operator, but you still have to turn up. The owner is the person whose business runs without them, and whose time goes on direction. The sooner you start that journey, the better.

What we work on

You, your business and your money

You work alongside other operators, with Adrian teaching from the front and working the room.

You

The plan

Mindset, your personal vision and a strategic map for the business. Then taking back your time: delegate and elevate.

Your business

Team, sales and systems

Marketing and sales that bring the right customers, SOPs so the business can run without you, and understanding how you and your team work.

Your money

Numbers, growth and AI

Knowing what makes the profit, growing organically and by acquisition, and putting AI to work in every seat.

  • A plan for the next three years, for the business and for you.
  • An operating rhythm, so the team can run the week.
  • Clear numbers on what makes the profit and what makes the business worth more.
  • A route to growth, organic and by acquisition.
  • AI working in every seat, not just a chatbot on the website.
  • A peer group of operators who will hold you to it.
